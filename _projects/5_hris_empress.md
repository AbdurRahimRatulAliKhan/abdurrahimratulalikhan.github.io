## Overview

Served as **Second Team Lead** for the EMPRESS HRIS platform at **Azolution Software and Engineers Ltd.**, delivering enterprise-grade human resource management features to major Bangladeshi corporations.

## Modules Developed

- **Recruitment Module** — End-to-end applicant tracking and onboarding
- **Vehicle Module** — Fleet management and scheduling
- **Canteen Module** — Cafeteria management with SMS integration
- **SMS Gateway Service** — Automated notifications
- **Email Service** — Automated email workflows
- **Auto Sale & Collection Service** — Automated financial service integrations

## Clients

Bashundhara Group · Modhumoti Bank PLC · NRBC Bank PLC · Renata

`.NET Framework 4.5` `Crystal Report` `Telerik Kendo UI` `amCharts` `jQuery` `MS SQL Server` `Three-Tier Architecture`
